HARDWARE ARCHITECTURE
• Microcontroller: ESP32-C3 RISC-V 32-bit Single-Core @ 160MHz
• Display Panel: 1.3" 240×240 IPS TFT (ST7789 via LovyanGFX High-Speed SPI)
• Input Engine: TTP223 Capacitive Touch ISR with Hardware Edge Debounce
• Power Subsystem: 1S LiPo Battery with 16-Sample Calibrated ADC & Standby Optimization
• Wireless: Bluetooth Low Energy 5.0 (NimBLE Sequential GATT Engine)

INSTALL AS STANDALONE APP

Android (Chrome / Edge / Samsung Internet):
1. Tap browser menu (3 dots in top right).
2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

iOS (Safari):
1. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari.
2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
Note: For Web Bluetooth on iOS, launch inside the Bluefy browser.

Desktop (Chrome / Edge):
Click the Install icon on the right side of the address bar.

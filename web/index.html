HARDWARE ARCHITECTURE
• Microcontroller: ESP32-C3 RISC-V 32-bit Single-Core @ 160MHz
• Display Panel: 1.3" 240×240 IPS TFT (ST7789 via LovyanGFX High-Speed SPI)
• Input Engine: TTP223 Capacitive Touch ISR with Hardware Edge Debounce
• Power Subsystem: 1S LiPo Battery with 16-Sample Calibrated ADC & Standby Optimization
• Wireless: Bluetooth Low Energy 5.0 (NimBLE Sequential GATT Engine)
